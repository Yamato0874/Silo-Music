Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Silo Player ("the app", "we") is a free, open-source desktop music player. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: we do not collect, receive, store or sell any personal data. Everything stays on your computer.

1. We have no servers and no accounts

The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no telemetry. The developer operates no server that receives your data. We cannot see what you listen to, search for, or save.

2. Data stored locally on your device

The app keeps the following in a local database in your user profile folder (on Windows: %APPDATA%\app.silo.player.native). It is never uploaded by the app:

  • your settings, chosen genres and favourite artists;
  • listening history, likes, dislikes and bans;
  • your playlists and downloaded-track records;
  • the API keys you enter (Jamendo, YouTube, SoundCloud);
  • a cache of search results, including results returned by the YouTube API Services, kept for up to 3 days to save API quota, and automatically deleted after 14 days at most;
  • automatic backup copies of the database.

You can delete all of this at any time by uninstalling the app and deleting that folder, or by using "Reset taste" and "Clear history" inside the app.

3. YouTube API Services

In the default yt-dlp library mode the app asks YouTube directly through the yt-dlp program installed by the user: search text and video ids are sent to YouTube from your computer, no API key is used, and the official player is not used. In the Pure YouTube mode the app uses YouTube API Services (the YouTube Data API v3) to search for videos and playlists, and the YouTube IFrame Player API to play videos. In the Pure YouTube mode the app does not download, separate or modify YouTube content. When you use YouTube features, you are agreeing to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Requests to YouTube are made directly from your computer, using the API key that you provide. Your search text is sent to YouTube to obtain results.
  • The embedded YouTube player is provided by YouTube and may set cookies or collect information as described in the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive that information.
  • The app does not access your Google account and does not ask you to sign in to Google.
  • You can learn how Google handles your data and revoke access that you have granted to any application at any time through the Google security settings page.

4. Other services

When you enter the corresponding keys, the app sends search requests directly from your computer to Jamendo and SoundCloud, and artist-name suggestions to MusicBrainz (with a generic app User-Agent and no personal identifiers). Each service handles requests under its own privacy policy. SoundCloud tracks play in SoundCloud's own embedded player.

5. Sharing and sale of data

Because we do not receive your data, we do not share, sell, rent or disclose it to anyone.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

7. Security

Data lives in your operating system user profile and is protected by your device's own access controls. API keys are stored in the local database as entered; please keep your computer and profile secure and do not share the database file.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.

9. Contact

Questions or requests: please open an issue at github.com/Yamato0874/Silo-Music/issues.
